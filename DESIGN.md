# Fischer Product Lab — visual language

Portfolio surface for the lab. This is a cinematic, read-only index of public demonstrations — not a night SaaS, not a landscape template, not a vibe-coding agency.

Steal the TrustDesk / Bridgemind **register**, not the IP: void, one light, grain, grotesque headline, real product chrome under the light.

## Tokens

| Token | Hex | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Navy | `#0B1220` | Void. Page, canvas, chrome. |
| Ivory | `#F4EFE4` | Type and the primary CTA fill. |
| Gold | `#C4A35A` | The only accent. Rules, meta, rim-light, secondary CTA. |

Atmosphere is one thing: **film grain + a single gold/ivory light** sitting over the featured product window. Status colors exist only inside product chrome, where they mean something.

## Type

Face: **Geist**. Mono: **Geist Mono**. Loaded via `next/font`.

Four sizes, no more:

| Name | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Display | Hero headline only |
| Title | Section heads |
| Body | Subcopy and product taglines |
| Meta | Eyebrows, nav, footer — mono, tracked, uppercase |

## Hero

- Fischer Product Lab as a **security and AI product laboratory**.
- Subcopy: public, synthetic, read-only enterprise demonstrations. Portfolio credibility.
- Two CTAs: jump to the product index, and open the featured live demo (TrustDesk).
- Featured window: TrustDesk’s real chrome (Northwind questionnaire), linking to the live TrustDesk URL in `app/products.ts`.

## Product index

Keep every product in `app/products.ts`. Restyle as monuments in this system — numbered rows, navy / ivory / gold — not a logo marquee. Every row still opens its live destination. GitHub stays secondary.

- No Inter as the face.

- Do not invent products, metrics, emails, or socials. Keep `hello@fischerproductlab.com` unless a confirmed replacement is already in the repo.
